MyGod is a local music player for macOS. Pick a folder and your collection is ready to play; flip one switch and AI assistants like WorkBuddy can search, skip and queue tracks for you. Your music is read straight from your Mac — what plays is always up to you.

FEATURES

Listening shouldn't be this much work

No importing your music into a library, and no new set of controls to learn just to play a song.

♫

Just pick a folder

Open the app, click "Open Folder" and choose where your music lives. MyGod reads the files in place and leaves your originals untouched.

✦

Let AI play it

Turn on "Allow Agent to connect to local MCP" in Status, and assistants like WorkBuddy can search, play and manage the queue for you.

◫

Everything on one screen

Track title, previous / next, pause, repeat, shuffle, progress and volume — all in a single window.

MADE FOR LISTENING

14 capabilities, all for listening to a song

Together these 14 capabilities cover the whole path from finding a track to shaping the sound the way you like it. They're not a developer API list — every one of them is about listening: finding music, queuing it, playing it, reading lyrics, and tuning the sound.

◎

Find it

Register a local folder, search by title or artist, open album and playlist pages; whether a track can play is shown right in the results.

▤

Queue it

Drop in a set of tracks, a whole album or a playlist; shuffle, repeat-all and repeat-one are one tap away.

♫

Control it

Play / pause / previous / next / jump to any track / seek / volume — several actions can be applied at once.

✎

Follow along

Timed lyrics, translations and word-by-word lyrics; plus a snapshot of what's playing, what's next and which sound settings are on.

♬

Shape the sound

A 10-band EQ, built-in presets, your own presets, night mode, loudness normalization, bass/treble enhancement — detailed below.

↻

Learn your taste

Likes, playlists and scenes are coming in later releases, so "just play something I like" keeps getting better.

Sound, in particular: the EQ and everything around it

Volume is only the beginning. MyGod also lets you decide how the music actually sounds: from a ten-band equalizer to a late-night mode, and once you've got it right you can save it as your own preset — it'll still be there next time you open the app. All of it exists purely for listening, not for configuration or development.

HOW IT WORKS

Two ways to use it

Tap through it yourself, or hand the song-finding to an AI assistant — no settings to change and no commands to remember.

① Just play my own collection

WHAT YOU DO

  1. Open the app, click "Open Folder" and choose your music directory;
  2. Hit play in the Player tab, then skip, repeat or adjust volume with the controls;
  3. Want a different set of songs? Click "Open Folder" again.
Playing right after you pickNo library import, no changes to your files

② Let an AI assistant do the playing

WHAT YOU DO

  1. Tick "Allow Agent to connect to local MCP" in the Status tab;
  2. Tell an MCP-capable assistant like WorkBuddy what you want to hear;
  3. It searches, builds the queue and starts playback — after that, one sentence is enough to skip.

Install in three steps

Drag MyGod into Applications

Open the downloaded dmg and drag the MyGod icon onto the Applications shortcut.

Click "Open Anyway" the first time

The current build isn't notarized by Apple, so macOS will stop it once. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway"; after that, double-clicking works normally.

Pick your music folder and listen

Open the app, click "Open Folder" and choose your music directory. To let AI help, switch it on in the Status tab.

If it still won't open, run the command below in Terminal and double-click again.

xattr -cr /Applications/MyGod.app
Apple Silicon Macs only for now (M-series); Intel Macs are not supported yet. A build you could simply double-click without step 2 would require an Apple Developer ID and notarization, which isn't available yet.
FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Why does macOS block it the first time?

The current build isn't notarized by Apple, so Gatekeeper stops it once. Click "Open Anyway" in System Settings → Privacy & Security, or run xattr -cr /Applications/MyGod.app in Terminal and double-click again. You only need to do this once.

Will my music be uploaded or changed?

No. The app only reads the folder you chose, in read-only mode, and never modifies or copies your originals. The current release plays local files only and makes no network calls. Track, artist and album names are shown exactly as they are in your files.

How do I let an AI assistant control playback?

Tick "Allow Agent to connect to local MCP" in the Status tab, then just tell an MCP-capable assistant like WorkBuddy what you want to hear. You can turn the switch off at any time; manual playback is unaffected.

Will online music sources be supported?

Yes — it's in preparation. The online edition coming soon is planned to add NetEase Cloud Music, QQ, Migu and Kuwo, so local and online music can be used together. The version you can download today still plays local files only, with no network access.

Does it work on Intel Macs?

Not yet. The current release supports Apple Silicon (M-series) Macs only; Intel support is planned.

Which languages does the interface support?

Simplified Chinese, English and Japanese, following your system language; any other language falls back to English.
