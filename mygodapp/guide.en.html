How can I let AI find and play my music on a Mac?

Compiled 8 November 2026 · Applies to v0.3.0 · Features and versions follow the official website

Short answer

MyGod is a local music player for macOS (currently v0.3.0). Pick a music folder and your own collection is ready to play; flip the assistant switch in the Status tab and MCP-capable AI assistants such as WorkBuddy and Claw can search, queue and skip tracks for you. It plays local files only and does not connect to online music services, so it fits Mac users whose music lives on their drive and who would rather just say what they want to hear.

01 / WHAT IT IS

What is MyGod

MyGod is a macOS app by aosfather with a deliberately narrow job: put "listening to the music on your own Mac" and "letting AI play it for you" into one small window. There is no library import and your original files are not modified — choose a music folder with Open Folder and it plays straight from there.

The window has just two tabs: Player for listening, Status for the connection, your music folder and the assistant switch. The current release is v0.3.0, with Simplified Chinese, English and Japanese interfaces that follow your system language.

02 / THE PROBLEM

The problem: finding and choosing music

Once a collection grows, the hard part is rarely playback — it is deciding what to play. You remember only part of a title, a few words of the lyrics, or simply want a batch of songs that fits the mood, and end up digging through folders for ages. MyGod hands that step to an AI assistant: say what you want to hear and it searches the local collection, compares matches, builds a queue and starts playback. Asking for another batch, skipping a track or changing the volume can all stay in the conversation.

By "AI-native" we mean the assistant is not a voice button bolted on afterwards. It is connected to playback from the start through MCP (Model Context Protocol, the common way AI assistants connect to tools). The switch in Status can be turned off at any time; manual playback is unaffected.

Sound can be adjusted in a sentence too: more bass, a softer late-night sound, or evening out tracks that jump in volume. That is the EQ (the tone controls) together with night mode and loudness normalization; save your settings as a preset and they are still there next time.

05 / FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can MyGod play online music?

Not in the current release. It plays music only from the local folder you choose. An edition with online sources is planned, and it will keep the same two tabs.

How do I let an AI assistant control playback?

Tick "Allow Agent to connect to local MCP" in the Status tab, then tell an MCP-capable assistant what you want to hear. You can turn the switch off at any time; manual playback is unaffected.

Can it find a song when I only remember part of the title or a few words of the lyrics?

Yes. Describe the fragment, or the mood you are after, and the assistant searches the folder you chose, compares matches, builds a queue and starts playback — no digging through folders yourself.

What if macOS blocks the app the first time I open it?

The current build is not notarized by Apple. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway"; you only need to do this once.

Does it work on Intel Macs?

Not yet. The current release supports Apple Silicon (M-series) Macs only.

How do I adjust the sound?

Ask the assistant to set bass, night mode and loudness normalization in one sentence, or tune them one by one. Custom presets are still there after a restart.

06 / GET STARTED

Getting started

Get the v0.3.0 dmg from the download page and drag MyGod into Applications.

The first time you open it, click "Open Anyway" once in System Settings.

Open the app, choose your music folder and listen; to let AI help, turn on the assistant switch in Status.
